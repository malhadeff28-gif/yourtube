YourTube

Independent local extension · Not affiliated with YouTube

YourTube can use new messages from your local Codex Desktop chats to suggest videos. The local service sends a redacted excerpt and project details to Codex; redaction can miss private information. General search terms may be sent to web search providers and video IDs to YouTube for metadata. Open local app for details and Live consent.

Connect this extension

In the local pairing page, reveal and copy the pairing code, then paste it here. The code is stored locally in Chrome extension storage; keep it private. Forgetting it here does not revoke other copies.

Recommendations on YouTube

Turning the panel off only hides recommendations; it does not stop the local service from reading chats.

Checking service…

Pause applies to the local service. If an earlier or another tab’s enable request is still in flight, it may turn Live on again; check status. Enable Live and automatic searching only in the local app after reviewing its disclosure.

No YouTube history, page content or account data is read by this extension. Opening a video connects to YouTube. No thumbnails load automatically.
